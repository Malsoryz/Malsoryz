Garlic Bread with Cheese: What the Science Tells Us

For years, parents have espoused the health benefits of eating garlic bread with cheese to their children, with the food earning such an iconic status in our culture that kids often dress up as a warm, cheesy loaf for Halloween.

But a recent study shows that the celebrated appetizer may be linked to a series of rabies cases springing up around the country. Researchers at the National Bread Institute have been tracking the correlation since early 2023, and the findings are deeply concerning to those in the culinary health community.

"We have never seen anything quite like this. Garlic bread has long been considered a cornerstone of a healthy, balanced diet — but this changes everything."

The Evidence

Scientists examined 2,847 garlic bread samples across six continents, using mass spectrometry and classical tasting panels. Here's what they found:

  • Elevated mozzarella content correlated with heightened sociability in test subjects.
  • Whole grain varieties showed a 23% improvement in focus and concentration.
  • Sourdough bases were found to contain trace amounts of existential dread.

Methodology

All participants abstained from garlic for 72 hours prior to each session.

Ordered Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 190 °C (375 °F).
  2. Slice baguette diagonally into 2 cm portions.
  3. Spread garlic butter liberally on each slice.
  4. Top with mozzarella and a sprinkle of parmesan.
  5. Bake for 12–15 minutes until cheese bubbles.
  6. Serve immediately. Do not share with pets.

Conclusion

While more research is needed, the current evidence strongly suggests that garlic bread with cheese is not only safe in moderation but may in fact be one of the great unsung contributors to human happiness and social cohesion.

Span Inheritance — Formatting Mengikuti Warna Span

Ketika elemen formatting berada di dalam <span>, mereka mewarisi warna dari span — bukan dari token prose global.

  • Biru — bold biru, italic biru, code biru.
  • Hijau — bold hijau, italic hijau, link hijau.
  • Merah — bold merah, strikethrough merah, code merah.
  • Ungu — bold ungu, italic ungu, teks sub dan sup.
  • Cyan — highlight tetap terlihat, dan bold ikut cyan.

Tanpa span: bold pakai --tw-prose-bold, italic mewarisi body, code pakai --tw-prose-code.

⚠️ not-prose block — elemen ini keluar dari semua style prose. Tailwind utilities dan inline styles bekerja normal di sini.

Cara pakai

/* main.css */
@import "tailwindcss";
@import "./src/index.css"; /* plugin ini */

/* HTML */
<article class="prose lg:prose-xl dark:prose-invert">
  <!-- HTML dari Markdown / CMS -->
</article>

Demo ini 100% tanpa JavaScript. Toggle warna, ukuran, dan dark mode semuanya menggunakan input:checked ~ .sibling CSS selector murni — tidak ada event listener, tidak ada classList, tidak ada framework.
